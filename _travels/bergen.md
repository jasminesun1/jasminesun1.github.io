  ## Logistics
  - Stayed at Bohemian hotel booked through Airbnb

  ## Itinerary

  ### 1/30
  - Landed at BGO and took train to Bergen
  - Pastries from Omni Kaiser (rec from Airbnb host)
  - Explored/shopped around Bryggen and town area
  - Folk & Rovere for beer and board game
  - Mount Floyen tram for the view + some walking trails
  - Trekroneren reindeer hotdog
  - Salong Bar & Billiard for some pool

  ### 1/31
  - Pastries from Omni Kaiser again, split a skolebroller from Kanelsnurren
  - Fjord boat tour
  - Fishmarket
  - More souvenir shopping + hot dog
  - Dinner at Olivia
  - Raevedilter bar (local college bar)

  ### 2/1
  - Mandelboller from Godt Brod
  - Train to airport and flew out

  ## Must Dos/Notes
  - Rocher croissant from Omni Kaiser
  - Fjord tour
  - Trekroneren hotdog: try the reindeer, but their other sausages are really good too
  - Any of the boller from Godt Brod
  - Sit window seat on the plane
