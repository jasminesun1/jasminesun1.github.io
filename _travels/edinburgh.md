  ## Logistics
  - Stayed at Stay Central in the City Center

  ## Itinerary

  ### 2/25
  - Landed at EDI and took tram to city center
  - Saw The News Steps, Edinburgh Castle (didn't go in), The Royal Mile
  - Breakfast at Blank Street
  - Victoria Street, Grassmarket shopping, Vennel View Point
  - Tom Riddell Grave, National Museum of Scotland
  - Arthur's Seat hike
  - Pastry from The Milkman
  - Scott Monument, St. Andrew Square Garden, New Town for shopping
  - Circus Lane
  - Dean Village
  - Tron Kirk Market
  - Mary's Milk Bar for gelato
  - Makar's Mash Bar for dinner
  - Department of Magic potion making experience

  ### 2/26
  - Breakfast from The Little Larder
  - Swanston Farm for Highland Cows
  - Lunch at The Elephant House
  - Tram to Airport

  ## Must Dos/Notes
  - National Museum of Scotland is free and has a rooftop terrace for views of the city and surrounding hills
  - Hike up to see the cows: there are some at the bottom of the hill, but if you hike up a mile or so, there are cows you can go up to
  - The Elephant House for Butterbeer
