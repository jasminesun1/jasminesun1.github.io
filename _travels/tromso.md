guide: |
  ## Logistics
  - Stayed at AirBnb near Trykkbassenget bus stop / 20 min walk outside of town

  ## Itinerary

  ### 2/12
  - Landed at TOS and took bus to AirBnb
  - Dinner at Dragøy, got fish & chips and salmon sashimi
  - <a href="https://chasinglights.com/tour/northern-lights-big-bus-chase/" target="_blank">Northern Lights Tour</a>

  ### 2/13
  - <a href="https://booking.bestarctic.com/en/to-do/2749659/reindeer-feeding-and-sami-culture/showdetails" target="_blank">Reindeer Feeding and Sami Culture Tour</a>
  - Explored downtown shops
  - Fish soup from Skarven Kro
  - Reindeer hot dog and mulled wine from Raketten
  - Northernmost McDonald's

  ### 2/14
  - Sauna & cold plunge at pust
  - Brunch at Risø Mat & Kaffebar
  - Bus to airport and flew out

  ## Must Dos/Notes
  - Northern lights tour
  - Reindeer tour, although the reindeer stew they feed you after you feed the reindeer isn't very good and also is a bit disturbing
  - pust, but bring flip flops so you aren't walking across snow barefoot...
