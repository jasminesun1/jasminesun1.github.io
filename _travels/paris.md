  ## Logistics
  - Stayed at Le Clark Hotel in the 3rd/11th arrondissement, near Le Marais

  ## Itinerary

  ### 2/14
  - Landed at ORY and ubered to hotel
  - Explored some vintage stores in Le Marais
  - Valentine's Dinner at L'Ami Jean
  - Eiffel Tower from Rue de L'Université and Jardin de la Tour Eiffel

  ### 2/15
  - Pastries from Boulangerie Utopie
  - Stumbled across a vintage pop up
  - Eiffel Tower view from Avenue de Camoens
  - Louvre
  - Late lunch at Brasserie Rosie
  - Transfer to BVA and fly out

  ## Must Dos/Notes
  - Ubering out of the airport may be cheaper than taking the train (which costs 14 euros per person)
  - I do not recommend BVA airport
  - Rue de Camoens isn't super crowded and has amazing views of the Eiffel Tower
  - Brasserie Rosie. Get the Escargot and Basque Cheesecake
